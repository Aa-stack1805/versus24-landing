The features that matter to you

Strava's data. Whoop's recovery. One app.

HR zone targeting

Z1 through Z5 set from lab tests or HRmax estimate. watchOS gives you live HR + zone during the workout.

Threshold + Z2 splits

Time in zone per session, weekly distribution, polarized vs pyramidal training balance.

Strava + Garmin import

Five years in one tap. Activity → effective load. Your history becomes a baseline, not just memory.

ACWR for endurance

Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio tuned for running and cycling. Flag overreach 2 weeks before it becomes injury.

Taper + race week

Tell Sensei your race date. It backs out the taper, the peak block, and adjusts as readiness shifts.

Apple Watch native

Live HR, lap splits, interval prompts. Saves back to phone, syncs to Strava if you keep both.
