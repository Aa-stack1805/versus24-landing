You came here because

Your sport is the training. Other apps ignore that.

  • Strava is built for runners. Hevy is built for lifters. Neither maps a 90-minute basketball practice to weekly training load.
  • You're in-season. Your lifting should periodise around games, not the other way around, and no lifting app does that.
  • You need a single number that says "you're peaking for Saturday" or "you're cooked, take Wednesday off". Readiness does that. Nothing else you've tried does.

The features that matter to you

Built for the season, not the workout.

Logger for your sport

Basketball, soccer, hockey, rugby, baseball, volleyball, tennis, cricket, lacrosse, American football, badminton, handball, and more. sRPE + duration in under 20 seconds.

Per-sport skill tree

14 sport disciplines mapped to actual training elements. Tag what you drilled: sprints, set pieces, court time. See where your work is concentrated.

Peak for game day

Readiness model knows your game date and adjusts the week's recommendations. Two readiness scores under 60? Sensei flags it before you blow Saturday.

In-season ACWR

Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio across your sport + lifting. A widely-used in-season workload signal. Core tier.

Periodised lifting

Pre-season, in-season, post-season: three different lifting modes. Volume and intensity adjusted to where you are in the season, not where Strong thinks you are.

Apple Watch native

Start a session from the watch during practice. Live HR, session save when you reconnect. Works standalone if your phone's in the locker.
