What you are paying for

A month of Elite costs less than one session with a coach.

  • You already own the sensor. Versus reads the Apple Watch on your wrist. Nothing to buy, no hardware subscription, nothing extra to charge at night. The recovery platforms answering the same question sell you the band first.
  • It is not trying to replace your coach. A coach watches you move and fixes what no sensor can see, and that is worth paying for. Versus covers the other days: the ones between sessions, when nobody is watching and you still have to decide what to do. It asks whether you have a coach for each sport, and if you do, it narrows itself to homework rather than programming over the top of them.
  • It replaces a stack, not an app. A lifting app, a running app, a recovery app, a nutrition app and a note somewhere with your fight camp in it, none of which can read each other. One subscription, one history, one number in the morning.
  • The free tier is not a countdown. New accounts start on Core for 14 days so you can see the analysis layer, and then drop to Free rather than to a paywall. All five loggers, daily readiness, the skill tree, the calendar, imports and the Watch app stay free after that, with no card taken and nothing auto-charging. Pay when the analysis or the coach is worth it to you, not to unlock your own data.
