The features that matter to you

Strava's data. Whoop's recovery. One app.

HR zone targeting

Z1 through Z5 set from lab tests or HRmax estimate, so Z2 base work, threshold efforts and VO₂max intervals are logged as what they were rather than as an undifferentiated run. watchOS gives you live HR and zone during the workout.

Route, distance, elevation

Outdoor runs and rides trace a GPS route, measured for distance and elevation and drawn on a map, with the line coloured by pace so you can see where you faded. Location is read only while an outdoor session is running.

Threshold + Z2 splits

Time in zone per session, weekly distribution, polarized vs pyramidal training balance.

Strava + Garmin import

Five years in one tap. Activity → effective load. Your history becomes a baseline, not just memory.

Load vs your own baseline

Runs and rides are weighted into a workload total and compared against your own recent norm, so a hard block shows up as a rising line rather than a surprise. Hard running is tracked separately from easy volume, so a spike in intensity does not hide inside a normal-looking week. It flags the ramp, not an injury, and reads best as one input among several.

Taper + race week

Tell Sensei your race date. It backs out the taper, the peak block, and adjusts as readiness shifts.

Apple Watch native

Live HR, lap splits, interval prompts. Saves back to phone, syncs to Strava if you keep both.
