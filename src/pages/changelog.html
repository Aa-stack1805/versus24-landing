v1.1 Shipped

Train Together, the Physique Report Card, and Nutrition.

  • Train Together: live joint resistance sessions. Shared template, separate weights, partner's sets appear as they finish them.
  • Physique Report Card (Elite): 0 to 100 score, per-muscle grades and ranked priorities, from your check-in photos, 12 measurements, frame and FFMI. Score history tracks across reports.
  • Regional fat distribution: where you store fat first and lean out last.
  • Nutrition Logger: meals, calories and macros, with targets from your own stats. Water, fiber, sugar and sodium included
  • AI Meal Scan (Elite): log a meal by photo, voice, or text, with no cap
  • Vacation Mode: mark planned time away so the break counts as intentional
  • Voice logging for strength: record exercises, sets, and intensity by voice
  • Sports Logger upgrade: faster sport selection, session controls, game results, score tracking, improved session recovery
  • Vanity Vault: FFMI and skeletal frame insights. Guided capture ghosts your last shot so angles match
  • Bug fixes and overall polish
