v1.1 Shipped

Train Together, the Physique Report Card, and Nutrition.

  • Train Together: run a live session with a training partner. Pick one of your templates as the shared structure and they get a push; you both land in the resistance logger with the same exercises, each logging your own weights. Their sets appear on your screen as they finish them, with side-by-side progress and a live in-sync status.
  • Physique Report Card (Elite): an honest judge's read on where you actually are. It weighs your check-in photos against your previous ones, all 12 measurements, your frame and FFMI, then returns a 0 to 100 score, per-muscle-group grades, your proportions against classic targets, what changed since last time, and a numbered list of what to fix first. Score history tracks across reports.
  • Regional fat distribution: where you store fat first and where you lean out last, so you know what to expect on the way down instead of guessing.
  • Nutrition Logger: track meals, calories, and daily macros alongside your training. Targets auto-calculated from your stats and goal; water, fiber, sugar, and sodium included
  • AI Meal Scan (Elite): log a meal by photo, voice, or text, with no cap
  • Vacation Mode: mark planned time away from training so Versus knows the break is intentional
  • Voice logging for strength: record exercises, sets, and intensity by voice
  • Sports Logger upgrade: faster sport selection, session controls, game results, score tracking, improved session recovery
  • Vanity Vault: FFMI and skeletal frame insights, plus guided photo capture that ghosts your last shot so angles match
  • Bug fixes and overall polish
