How to read the numbers

What readiness and workload are, and what they aren't.

  • Readiness is an estimate. It's computed from your HRV and sleep in Apple Health plus your morning check-in, so it reads recovery rather than how hard the week has been. A guide for how to train today, not a verdict.
  • Workload is a training signal. Your recent training is measured against your own rolling baseline. It shows when volume is ramping quickly. Nothing more.
  • It does not diagnose or predict injury. Versus is a training-history and planning tool, not a medical device. It won't tell you whether you're hurt or about to be. Listen to your body and a qualified coach or clinician.
