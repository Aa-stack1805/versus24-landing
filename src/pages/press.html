About Versus Training

Versus Training is a training app built for athletes who train across modalities: combat sports, strength, endurance, sport, recovery. The app unifies what usually lives in five separate apps: sparring rounds, lifting sets (Hevy / Strong), endurance intervals (Strava), readiness (Whoop), and recovery (HealthKit). Plus an AI coach called Sensei, powered by Claude, that talks about your actual training, not generic plans.

Built and launched solo. iOS-first; Apple Watch native; Android planned for 2027.

Free tier, Core ($9.99/mo), Elite ($14.99/mo with Sensei AI).
