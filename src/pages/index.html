You came here because

Most apps only count one kind of training.

How to read the number

What readiness and workload are, and what they aren't.

  • Readiness is an estimate. It comes from your HRV, sleep and resting heart rate in Apple Health and from your morning check-in, compared against your own baseline. It measures how recovered you are, not how hard you have been training: a heavy week cannot push the score down on its own. A guide for how to train today, not a verdict on your body.
  • Workload counts every modality. Recent work is measured against your own baseline, not a population average. On most apps that baseline only sees one sport, which is why a heavy lifting week can hide inside a running app. Here the roll, the run and the squat session land in the same total.
  • Neither one diagnoses an injury or predicts one. Versus is a training log and a planning tool, not a medical device. The load trend tells you the ramp is steep; it does not tell you that you are hurt, and it is no substitute for noticing that you are.

The coach

It asks before it prescribes.

Sensei runs on Anthropic's Claude, with your last 30 sessions, your current camp, your readiness and your PRs in front of it. That is the whole trick: it answers about your training instead of about training. Ask it for an exercise swap and it wants to know why first. Tell it what happened in a round and it tags the positions and the finishes.
