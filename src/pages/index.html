How it works

Three steps. That's it.

01 / LOG IT

Speak it, import it, or tap.

Combat rounds, lifting sets, endurance intervals, mobility. Same app. Voice-parse a sparring session in 8 seconds, or import five years from Strava, Hevy, Strong, Fitbod, and Garmin in one tap.

02 / KNOW IT

One number every morning.

Versus reads your HealthKit data and gives you one readiness score. HRV, sleep, training load, your week's pattern, distilled into a number you can act on.

03 / IMPROVE IT

A coach who actually read your data.

Sensei is powered by Claude. It knows your fight record, camp, last week's sleep, and whether you're peaking or about to plateau. Voice in, structured plans out.

The 30-second log

Speak your workout. Watch it log itself.

Most apps make you tap through five screens to log a sparring session. Versus's Sensei understands what you just trained: discipline, rounds, techniques, intensity, even the way it felt. One voice log per day, free for everyone. Unlimited in Elite.
