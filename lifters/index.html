The features that matter to you

Hevy's logger. FitBod's intelligence.

PR auto-detection

Every set is checked. e1RM, 3RM, 5RM, volume PR. Celebrates the win. Opt-in to share to gym feed.

ACWR workload ratio (Plus)

Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio computed across all your training. A widely-used sports-science signal for whether your workload is ramping too fast.

Plateau detector

30-day window per movement. When you stall, Sensei suggests a deload, variation, or fatigue-management protocol.

Templates + supersets

Build once, run forever. Drop sets, supersets, RPE/RIR, rest timers, percentage-of-1RM auto-loading.

Imports

Five years from Hevy, Strong, Fitbod in one tap. Your PR history travels with you.

Body composition

12 measurements + AI body-fat estimate from progress photos. Trend lines, not vanity.
