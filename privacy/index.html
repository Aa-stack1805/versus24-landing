Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 21, 2026 · Applies to the Versus iOS application

This privacy policy applies to the Versus app (referred to as the "Application") for mobile devices, developed by Aadit Singal (referred to as the "Service Provider") as a freemium service. This service is provided "AS IS".

Information We Collect

The Application collects information you provide when you register and use it. This includes:

  • Email address
  • First and last name
  • Age (stored as approximate date of birth)
  • Gender

The Application also collects certain information automatically, including the type of mobile device you use, your device's unique ID, IP address, mobile operating system, browser type, and information about how you use the Application.

Health and Fitness Data

The Application reads health and fitness data from Apple HealthKit, including heart rate variability (HRV) and sleep data. This data is used solely to calculate your daily readiness score. Health data is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose other than delivering the readiness features within the Application. All health data processing is performed in accordance with Apple's HealthKit guidelines and applicable privacy laws.

How We Share Your Data with Other Users

The Application includes social features that share certain information with other users in your gym network and with users you have accepted as training partners. The following information may be visible to other users depending on your settings:

  • Gym feed (when enabled): Your workouts (modality, duration, sRPE, training load, notes, and feed caption), personal records (exercise name, weight, reps), photos attached to feed posts, comments, and reactions are visible to members of the gyms you join.
  • Training partners (when accepted): Your daily readiness score, latest session summary, and weekly training load (in arbitrary units) are visible to users you have accepted as training partners. During an active partner challenge, weekly training load is also visible to your opponent.
  • Profile identity: Your display name (first name), profile photo, and gym membership are shown alongside any content visible to other users.

You control what is shared through the privacy settings in the Application, including the "Share to gym feed" and "Share PRs" toggles and per-partner acceptance. Turning a setting off prevents future sharing; it does not retroactively remove content already posted (you can delete individual posts and PRs at any time).

Information shared via these social features is shared only with the other Versus users you have a relationship with through the Application (gym co-members or accepted partners). It is never sold or shared with advertisers.

User-Generated Content

The Application allows users to submit progress photos and record body measurements through the Vanity Vault feature, and to share workouts, photos, captions, comments, and reactions through the gym feed. Vanity Vault content is private to you and stored securely in the Service Provider's cloud infrastructure; it is not shown to other users. Gym feed content is shared with other users as described in the section above. None of this content is sold to third parties or used for advertising purposes.

You can delete any individual post, comment, photo, or measurement from within the Application at any time. You can also report or block other users from the same interface; see the "Content Moderation and Safety" section below.

Content Moderation and Safety

To keep the gym feed safe, the Application provides in-app tools to report any post or comment you find objectionable and to block any other user. Reports are reviewed by the Service Provider within 24 hours. When you block a user, you no longer see their posts, comments, or reactions, and they no longer see yours. The Service Provider may also remove content or suspend accounts that violate the Terms & Conditions. We retain a record of reports, blocks, and moderation actions for safety and abuse-prevention purposes for as long as is reasonably necessary.

Push Notifications

If you grant permission, the Application sends push notifications through the Apple Push Notification Service (APNs). Notifications may be triggered by events such as a training partner logging a workout, a partner-challenge result, a new reaction or comment on your feed posts, or a partner invite. We store an opaque device token in our infrastructure so notifications can be routed to your device, and we log delivery metadata for rate-limiting and reliability purposes. You can disable push notifications at any time from iOS Settings → Notifications → Versus.

Account Discoverability

Other Versus users may look up your account by your email address in order to send you a training-partner invite. Lookups are rate-limited (currently 20 per user per day) and return only your public profile (display name and profile photo). You cannot currently opt out of email lookup; deleting your account removes you from this index.

Artificial Intelligence

The Application uses AI technologies to enhance user experience through the Sensei coaching feature. AI components may process your training history, readiness data, session logs, fight records, voice transcripts, and (where you opt in) progress photos to generate personalised coaching advice, session plans, sparring analysis, and body-composition estimates. Specifically:

  • Personalised coaching responses based on your logged sessions and readiness score
  • AI-generated daily session recommendations based on your training history
  • Sparring debrief analysis based on post-session input
  • Opponent scouting and gameplan suggestions based on structured profile data
  • Structured session extraction from voice logging (see below)
  • Body-composition estimates from progress photos you explicitly submit for analysis (see below)

Sensei is powered by the Claude API operated by Anthropic. When you interact with Sensei, the relevant session context, chat messages, and (for voice features) text transcripts are transmitted to Anthropic for processing and returned to the Application. Anthropic acts as a data processor on the Service Provider's behalf.

Voice Logging. When you use the voice-logging feature, your speech is transcribed on-device by Apple's Speech framework. The resulting text transcript (not the original audio) is then sent to Anthropic's Claude API to extract structured session data such as exercise, duration, or perceived effort. The audio recording itself is not transmitted off-device and is not retained after on-device transcription.

AI Body-Composition Analysis. If you choose to use the optional body-composition estimate feature, the progress photo you submit is sent to an AI image-analysis service to produce an estimated body-fat percentage and related metrics. This is opt-in and only performed on photos you explicitly submit for analysis; photos you upload solely to the Vanity Vault are not analysed by AI. Estimates are approximate and are not a medical assessment.

All AI outputs are recommendations the user acts on voluntarily. No automated decisions with legal or significant personal effect are made by the Application. All AI processing is performed in accordance with this privacy policy and applicable laws.

Location Data

The Application does not collect precise or approximate location information from your device. The Application does not request location permissions.

Third Party Access

Only aggregated, anonymised data is periodically transmitted to external services to help improve the Application. The Service Provider may share your information with third parties as described in this policy.

The Application uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, cloud storage (including photo storage), and serverless functions
  • RevenueCat — subscription and payment management
  • Anthropic — operator of the Claude AI used by Sensei; receives session context, chat messages, and voice transcripts in order to generate coaching responses and structured session data
  • Sentry — error tracking and diagnostics
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Apple HealthKit — health and fitness data
  • Apple Push Notification Service (APNs) — delivery of push notifications to your device

The Service Provider may disclose your information:

  • As required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena or similar legal process
  • When disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud
  • With trusted service providers who work on our behalf, who have no independent use of the information disclosed to them, and who have agreed to adhere to this privacy policy

Opt-Out Rights

You can stop all collection of information by the Application by uninstalling it using the standard uninstall processes available on your device or via the App Store.

Data Retention

The Service Provider will retain user-provided data for as long as you use the Application and for a reasonable time thereafter. Automatically collected information is retained for up to 24 months and may thereafter be stored in aggregate form. If you would like your user data deleted, please contact us at aadit@versus24.net and we will respond within a reasonable time. Note that some data may be required for the Application to function properly.

Children's Privacy

The Application does not address anyone under the age of 13. The Service Provider does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal information, it will be immediately deleted from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at aadit@versus24.net.

Security

The Service Provider takes the confidentiality of your information seriously and provides physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect the information we process and maintain. Access to your information is limited to authorised employees and contractors who need it to operate, develop, or improve the Application. Please be aware that no security system can prevent all potential breaches.
