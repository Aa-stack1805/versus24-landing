v1.1 Shipped

Train Together, the Physique Report Card, and Nutrition.

  • Train Together: live joint resistance sessions. Shared template, separate weights, partner's sets appear as they finish them.
  • Physique Report Card (Elite): 0 to 100 score, per-muscle grades and ranked priorities, from your check-in photos, 12 measurements, frame and FFMI. Score history tracks across reports.
  • Regional fat distribution: where you store fat first and lean out last.
  • Nutrition Logger: meals, calories and macros, with targets from your own stats. Water, fiber, sugar and sodium included
  • AI Meal Scan (Elite): log a meal by photo, voice, or text, with no cap
  • Vacation Mode: mark planned time away so the break counts as intentional
  • Voice logging for strength: record exercises, sets, and intensity by voice
  • Sports Logger upgrade: faster sport selection, session controls, game results, score tracking, improved session recovery
  • Vanity Vault: FFMI and skeletal frame insights. Guided capture ghosts your last shot so angles match
  • Bug fixes and overall polish
v1.2 · Planned Late 2026

The rings, rebuilt. Sensei reads the session.

  • Three rings, three separate questions: how much you did, how far that sits from your own normal, and how your body responded. Each ring now answers one of them instead of two of them blurred together.
  • Balance replaces the ratio: the inner ring reads full when every system you train sits near your own baseline, and drains only when one of them spikes. Rest days no longer empty it, because resting is not a failure state.
  • Readiness measures recovery, not work: the score is now built from how you slept, how you feel and what your HRV did. Training volume moved out to the load rings, so a hard week can no longer push your readiness down on its own.
  • Life stress counts: the morning check-in's stress slider now feeds the score, alongside energy and soreness.
  • Baselines that survive an outlier: one competition day or one wild week no longer drags your normal range with it.
  • AI coaching past the weights room: endurance, combat and sport sessions get a read from Sensei. Until now only resistance training did.
  • Endurance, prescribed: zone target, duration and effort arrive pre-set in the logger, so the goal ring and the zone HUD are armed before you start.
  • Combat and sport, Sensei's Read: an intensity ceiling, what to protect, what to recover. No techniques, no drills, no round structures. Your coach programs the session; the app carries the load history your coach cannot see.
  • Sport days schedule properly: a sport day in your weekly split now survives into the AI Daily Stack instead of being swapped for something else.
  • Export workout cards as social-ready images (IG Stories, TikTok)
  • Voice logging in Spanish + Portuguese
