v1.0 · Launch Shipped · May 2026

Live on the App Store.

  • Daily readiness from HealthKit (HRV + sleep + load + ACWR)
  • Voice session logging: unlimited for Elite, 1/day for Free + Core
  • Sensei AI chat, AI Sparring Debrief, Situation Drill, Opponent Scouting, AI Body Fat Estimate (Elite)
  • AI Daily Stack + AI Mobility Protocol (Core)
  • Training Camps, Fight Week, Vanity Vault
  • Gym feed + partner hub (opt-in)
  • Imports from Strava, Hevy, Strong, Fitbod, Garmin
  • Apple Watch native combat timer + live HR
v1.1 Shipped

Nutrition, Vacation Mode, and strength voice logging.

  • Nutrition Logger: track meals, calories, and daily macros alongside your training. Targets auto-calculated from your stats and goal; water, fiber, sugar, and sodium included
  • AI Meal Scan (Elite): log a meal by photo, voice, or text — unlimited
  • Vacation Mode: mark planned time away from training so Versus knows the break is intentional
  • Voice logging for strength: record exercises, sets, and intensity by voice
  • Sports Logger upgrade: faster sport selection, session controls, game results, score tracking, improved session recovery
  • Vanity Vault: FFMI and skeletal frame insights
  • Bug fixes and overall polish
