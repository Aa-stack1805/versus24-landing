About Versus Training

Versus Training is the first training app built for athletes who train across modalities — combat sports, strength, endurance, sport, recovery. The app unifies what currently lives in five separate apps: sparring rounds (no app handles them), lifting sets (Hevy / Strong), endurance intervals (Strava), readiness (Whoop), and recovery (HealthKit). Plus an AI coach called Sensei, powered by Claude, that talks about your actual training — not generic plans.

Built and launched solo. iOS-first; Apple Watch native; Android planned for 2027.

Free tier, Core ($12.99/mo), Elite ($19.99/mo with Sensei AI).
