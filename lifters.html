PR auto-detection

Every set is checked. e1RM, 3RM, 5RM, volume PR. Celebrates the win. Opt-in to share to gym feed.

ACWR injury risk (PRO)

Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio computed across all your training. The sports-science gold-standard for "are you overdoing it."
