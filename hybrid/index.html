You came here because

Other apps make you choose.

  • Strong logs your lifts. Strava logs your runs. Neither tells you when you're cooked from doing both this week.
  • The "AI coach" in every other app generates a generic workout. It doesn't know your Tuesday lift made today's run feel terrible.
  • ACWR is the standard injury-risk metric — across all modalities. No fitness app tracks it cross-modality. Versus Training does.

The features that matter to you

One app. Two modalities. Same readiness model.

Cross-modality ACWR

Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio computed across everything you train. Lift, combat, run, sport — same baseline, same risk model.

Two-sport Training Camp

Periodisation that knows you're lifting AND running. Volume and intensity adjusted across both, not stacked blindly.

Sensei multi-modality

Sensei sees your full week. "Should I run tomorrow or lift?" gets a real answer grounded in HRV, sleep, and what you trained.

Readiness, one number

Pulls from HealthKit. Same score whether you're a hybrid lifter+runner or BJJ+strength. The number that runs the whole app.

5 modality loggers

Resistance, combat, endurance, sport, mobility. All five available on day one. Switch in two taps.

Imports across the board

Strava, Hevy, Strong, Fitbod, Garmin. Bring five years of history from whichever silo your data is in.
