FAQ

Common questions. Honest answers.

If something here doesn't answer it, email aadit@versus24.net — replies within 24 hours.

Product

Do I need to be a fighter to use this?

No. Versus is built as a multi-modality athlete OS. Combat is the positioning hook, but the resistance, endurance, sport, and mobility loggers stand on their own. If you only lift, you can use it like Hevy. If you only run, you can use it like Strava (with better recovery science). The fighter audience just gets the deepest tooling.

Does it work for lifters?

Yes. The resistance logger handles supersets, drop sets, RPE/RIR, percentage-of-1RM auto-loading, and PR detection on every set. Imports from Hevy, Strong, and Fitbod bring your history with you. PRO adds ACWR injury risk + plateau detection.

Does it work for runners and cyclists?

Yes. The endurance logger uses HR zones (set from lab tests or HRmax estimate). Imports from Strava and Garmin bring five years of activity. PRO adds ACWR-for-endurance and race-week taper logic. watchOS gives live HR and lap splits.

Does it sync with Apple Health, Strava, Hevy, Garmin?

Apple Health: two-way sync (we read HRV, sleep, RHR, workouts; we write Versus sessions back). Strava + Garmin: one-tap import of your full history. Hevy, Strong, Fitbod: import only — we don't push back to those apps.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes. Native watchOS app. Combat round timer, live HR, lap splits for endurance, sRPE prompts at end of session. Saves back to the phone over WatchConnectivity before you towel off.

Is there an Android app?

Not at launch. iOS-first. Android is planned for 2027 once the iOS product proves out. If Android is a deal-breaker for you, drop your email on the waitlist anyway — we'll notify you when Android opens.

Does voice work for lifting?

Combat and endurance: yes, at launch. Resistance voice parsing is the most-requested v1.1 feature — shipping inside the first 30 days of launch. Until then, the resistance logger is tap-through but fast (templates + last-session recall).

Regardless of modality, voice session logging is one log per day on Free and PRO, unlimited on MAX.

Pricing

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — via the App Store, like every iOS subscription. Apple handles cancellations and the refund window. Your data stays in the app on Free tier after cancellation.

Is the free tier really free forever?

Yes. All 5 loggers, daily readiness + morning check-in, 90-day history, gym feed + partner hub + partner challenges, 14-discipline skill tree (starter belt), 3 workout templates, 8 Vanity Vault check-ins, imports from Strava / Hevy / Strong / Fitbod / Garmin, Apple Watch, and one voice session log per day. No ads. No card required.

What's the difference between PRO and MAX?

PRO ($12.99/mo or $99.99/yr) unlocks the full analytics + AI programming layer: ACWR injury chart, plateau detection, full load trends, 365-day history, Training Camps + Fight Week, the full skill tree (all belts across all 17 disciplines), unlimited Vault and templates, plus two AI features that prescribe your training — the AI Daily Stack (sessions planned each morning) and the AI Mobility Protocol (post-session mobility prescribed by AI).

MAX ($19.99/mo or $149.99/yr) adds the conversational coach. Sensei is a load-aware AI you talk to about your actual training. MAX also unlocks AI Sparring Debrief, Situation Drill + Opponent Scouting, AI Body Fat Estimate, and unlimited voice session logging (Free and PRO both get one voice log per day).

What happens if I'm on the waitlist?

30-day free trial of MAX, sent to your inbox on launch day. No card required for the trial — at the end you choose Free, PRO, or MAX.

Data + privacy

Where does my data live?

On your phone (SwiftData local-first) and in our Supabase database (encrypted in transit and at rest). HRV/sleep data stays on-device in HealthKit — Versus reads it but doesn't store it on our servers.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Settings → Account → Export. You get a CSV/JSON archive of every session, every set, every PR.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Settings → Account → Delete Account. Removes your profile, workouts, photos, feed posts, comments, partnerships, and gym memberships within 30 days. Aggregated, anonymized data may be retained for product improvement.

Sensei AI

Where does Sensei run?

On Anthropic's Claude. Your chat messages, the relevant session context, and voice transcripts (for voice logging) are sent to Anthropic to generate the response. Audio is not sent — transcription happens on-device via Apple's Speech framework. Full disclosure in the privacy policy.

Does Anthropic train on my data?

No. Versus uses Anthropic's API tier, which does not train on customer data by default.

What languages does voice work in?

English at launch (US + UK accents tested). Adding Spanish + Portuguese in v1.2.
